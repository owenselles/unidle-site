StayActive

Privacy Policy

StayActive does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, and no network connection.

What we collect

Nothing. StayActive has no servers and makes no network requests. The app is distributed without a network entitlement, so it is not technically capable of sending data anywhere, whether we wanted it to or not.

Specifically, StayActive does not

  • Collect or transmit personal information of any kind
  • Include analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising
  • Create an account or ask you to sign in
  • Read your screen contents, documents, or clipboard
  • Record, log, or observe your keystrokes or mouse input
  • Read or interact with any other application
  • Share anything with third parties, because there is nothing to share

Why StayActive asks for Accessibility access

macOS requires Accessibility permission before an app may signal input activity to the system. StayActive uses this permission for exactly one purpose: posting a mouse-moved event that carries no movement, which tells macOS that the machine is not idle.

The Accessibility permission on macOS is a single switch that also allows apps to read and control other applications. StayActive does not use it for that. It never inspects windows, reads text, observes input, or controls other apps. It only sends one outgoing event, on a timer you configure.

Your settings

Your preferences — whether the app is on, the idle threshold, and your work-hours schedule — are stored locally on your Mac in the app's own container using the standard macOS preferences system. They never leave your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

StayActive is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date below. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in practice.
