Unidle

Keep your Mac from going idle

Your Mac decides you're gone after a few minutes without a keystroke — not because you left, but because you were reading, presenting, or waiting on a long build.

Unidle keeps your Mac reporting itself as present, so it doesn't dim, sleep, or mark itself inactive while you're still sitting there. It lives in the menu bar, has no Dock icon, and stays out of the way.

What it does

  • Signals activity to macOS so your Mac never appears idle
  • Keeps the display awake without a separate sleep utility
  • Limits itself to your working hours and days, including overnight shifts
  • Lets you choose how long a pause should last before it steps in
  • Launches at login and stays out of your way

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no network access. Unidle never reads your screen, your keystrokes, or any other app. It ships without a network entitlement, so it cannot send data anywhere. See the privacy policy.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later. Unidle needs Accessibility permission, which macOS requires for any app that signals input activity. The support page walks through granting it.
